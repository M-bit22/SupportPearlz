SupportPearlz Customer Support Knowledge Base

Our customer support team is available Monday through Friday,
from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

Customers can contact support through support@example.com.

Standard order processing takes 2 to 3 business days.

Customers can request a refund within 14 days of receiving
their order, provided the product meets the refund conditions.

For technical support, customers should provide their order
number and a description of the issue.
