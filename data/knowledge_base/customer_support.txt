Pearl Store — Customer Support Reference
Knowledge-base document for SupportPearlz

SUPPORT HOURS
The customer support team is available Monday through Friday, from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

ORDER PROCESSING
Standard order processing takes 2–3 days. This is the processing estimate only; carrier transit time and total delivery time are not specified.

REFUNDS
Customers can request a refund within 14 days of receiving their order, provided the product meets the refund conditions. Detailed conditions are not listed in the supplied material.

CONTACT AND ESCALATION
A dedicated phone number, email address, WhatsApp number, and after-hours escalation procedure have not been provided. Do not invent contact details. For case-specific questions, direct customers to the store's official support channel.

IMPORTANT KNOWLEDGE GAPS
The supplied material does not specify:
- Detailed refund eligibility conditions or exceptions.
- A separate damaged/defective-item policy.
- Refund processing time.
- Shipping fees or international shipping availability.
- Carrier transit times or guaranteed delivery dates.
- Order cancellation or modification rules.

The assistant must clearly state when these details are not documented and must not guess.
